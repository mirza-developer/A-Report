Fonts in this folder are used by the unit tests only. They are not part of the NuGet packages.

AReportTestSans-Regular.ttf, AReportTestSans-Bold.ttf
    Subsets of DejaVu Sans (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io), renamed "AReport Test Sans" as the DejaVu license requires for
    modified versions. DejaVu fonts are (c) Bitstream, Inc. and (c) Tavmjong Bah, distributed under a permissive free license
    (Bitstream Vera License with public-domain DejaVu changes) that allows use, copying, modification and redistribution.

AReportTestCJK-Regular.ttf
    Generated by the A-Report authors from simple geometric shapes (no glyph outlines taken from any other font).
    It maps a few Chinese characters to distinctive boxes so tests can verify glyph mapping, embedding and line breaking
    without shipping a real CJK font.
